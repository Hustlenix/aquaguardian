Evidence-backed ocean context

Annual ocean plastic entry

About 11 million metric tons

UNEP and Pew estimate that roughly 11 million metric tons of plastic enter the ocean every year.

Source: UNEP / Pew Charitable Trusts

Protected ocean area

About 8.4%

Recent global assessments place marine protected areas at roughly 8.4% of the ocean.

Source: UNEP-WCMC / Protected Planet

Coral reef risk

70–90%

A large share of the world’s coral reefs are considered at risk from warming, acidification, and local stressors.

Source: UNEP / ICRI

Coastal population exposure

About 40%

Around 40% of the global population lives within 100 km of the coast, which makes coastal stewardship especially important.

Source: UNEP

Excess heat absorbed by the ocean

More than 90%

The ocean has absorbed more than 90% of the excess heat trapped in the Earth system by greenhouse gases, which drives sea-level rise, marine heatwaves, and coral bleaching.

Source: NOAA Climate.gov / NASA

Oxygen produced by the ocean

About 50%

Scientists estimate that roughly half of Earth’s oxygen production comes from the ocean, mostly from photosynthesizing plankton.

Source: NOAA Ocean Service

Ghost fishing gear in the GPGP

About 46% of mass

Abandoned fishing nets, lines, and ropes make up about 46% of the Great Pacific Garbage Patch’s mass — gear that keeps trapping marine life for years.

Source: Lebreton et al., Scientific Reports 2018
